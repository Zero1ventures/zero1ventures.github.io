What this demo shows

  • The assistant answers from the business's own documents and books real time slots from a calendar.
  • Times are offered as buttons; the assistant can only offer times the calendar actually has open.
  • Nothing is booked until the visitor taps Confirm on the summary card. The AI cannot book on its own.
  • Double bookings are blocked by the database, and addresses outside the service area are turned away.
  • Try: "Can I get a tune-up Tuesday morning?", "Do you service Orlando?" or "What does a repair visit cost?"
