How a plan comes together

  1. Start an event

    Name it, pick an emoji, and suggest a few dates. It takes about thirty seconds.

  2. Invite your group

    Share the code, a link, or a QR. Friends download HeyCrew and join from their own phones, no sign-up.

  3. Decide together

    Everyone marks when they're free and votes on ideas. The dates and picks that work for the most people rise to the top.

What's in every event

Availability calendar
Everyone marks the days they're free, and HeyCrew shows which dates work for the most people.
Ideas board
Save places to stay, eat, and things to do. The group votes and the favorites rise.
Polls

Turn the winning ideas into a day-by-day plan, and assign who's bringing what.
Event chat

Questions

Do my friends need the app?

Yes. HeyCrew is free on iOS and Android. Friends download it and join your event with the code or link you share.

Do I need to create an account?

No. HeyCrew sets you up automatically when you start or join an event. You can connect Apple or Google later to keep your events if you switch phones.

What happens to free events after 30 days?

Free events stay active for 30 days. HeyCrew+ events don't expire.

How do I cancel HeyCrew+?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. Cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

How do I get help?

Ask our help assistant with the Ask HeyCrew button, or email support@heycrewapp.com.
