What this concept adds

Prepared by Zero1 Ventures for Superior Flood, Inc. Try the assistant, then open the lead inbox to see what your team would get.

1Every visitor becomes a lead record

The moment someone types an address, a lead is created. Abandoned quotes are no longer lost; they become a follow-up list.

2Automatic FEMA flood zone lookup

Uses public FEMA flood map data to pre-qualify risk before an agent ever picks up the phone.

3Contact captured before the price

Name, email, and phone are collected with logged consent language, timestamped for TCPA records.

Each lead is scored (lender requirement, renewal date, current premium) so agents call the hottest leads first.

"Talk to an agent" is always visible. Production version texts and emails the on-call agent instantly.

6Guardrails built in

The assistant never invents prices or recommends coverage. Pricing comes only from the rating source; advice goes to licensed agents.
