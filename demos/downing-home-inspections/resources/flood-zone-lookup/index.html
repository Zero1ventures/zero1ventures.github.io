Central Florida flood zone lookup

Check a home's official FEMA flood zone, then call the county if you need help reading the map. Do this before you make an offer, because flood insurance can change your monthly cost.

What the zones mean

ZoneRiskWhat it means
A, AE, AH, AOHighSpecial Flood Hazard Area: at least a 1% chance of flooding each year. Flood insurance is required for most mortgages.
X (shaded)ModerateBetween the 1% and 0.2% annual chance floodplains. Insurance usually isn't required but is worth pricing.
X (unshaded)LowerOutside the mapped floodplain. Lower risk, not no risk.
DUnknownFlood risk hasn't been studied.

Before you make an offer

  • Look up the address yourself. Don't rely on a listing or a neighbor's zone, since lots on the same street can be in different zones.
  • Ask the seller about past flooding or water in the home, and whether they carry flood insurance.
  • Get a flood insurance quote early. Your lender can require it in any zone.
  • If the home is in a high-risk zone, ask whether an elevation certificate exists. It can affect the insurance price.

Most Central Florida flooding comes from heavy rain, not the coast. Being outside a mapped zone doesn't mean a home can't flood.
