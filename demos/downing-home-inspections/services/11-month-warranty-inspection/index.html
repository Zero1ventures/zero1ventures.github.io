11-Month Warranty Inspection in Orlando

Catch new-construction defects before your builder warranty expires.

Get instant quoteCall 727-243-4158

What it is

New homes have defects too. An 11-month inspection is a full inspection timed before your builder warranty ends, so you have a documented list of items for the builder to fix at their cost.

Who needs it

Owners of newly built homes approaching the end of the builder's one-year warranty.

What's included

  • Full home inspection scope
  • Settlement cracks, drainage and grading issues
  • Roof, attic insulation and ventilation
  • Plumbing, electrical and HVAC performance after a year of use
  • Report formatted so you can send it straight to the builder

Questions

When should I book it?

Around month 10 or 11, leaving enough time for the builder to respond before the warranty expires.

Didn't the county already inspect it?

Code inspections check minimum standards at points during construction. They're not a full condition inspection of the finished home.
