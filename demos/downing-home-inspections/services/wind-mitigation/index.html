Wind Mitigation Inspection in Orlando

Document your home's wind-resistant features so your insurer can apply credits.

What it is

A wind mitigation inspection records the features that help a home resist hurricane winds: roof shape, deck attachment, roof-to-wall connections, secondary water barrier and opening protection. Insurers use the form to apply wind-loss credits to your premium.

Who needs it

Florida homeowners renewing or shopping for insurance, and buyers who need a policy before closing.

What's included

  • Completed Uniform Mitigation Verification form (OIR-B1-1802)
  • Roof covering type and permit date
  • Roof deck attachment and roof-to-wall connection
  • Roof geometry (hip, gable, flat)
  • Secondary water resistance
  • Opening protection: shutters, impact windows and doors
  • Photo documentation your insurer can verify

Questions

How much will it save me?

It depends on your home and your insurer. Homes with hip roofs, strapping and impact protection usually see the biggest credits. Ask your agent what credits your carrier offers.

How long is a wind mitigation report valid?

Florida wind mitigation forms are generally accepted for up to five years, as long as nothing on the home has changed.

Can I bundle it with a 4-point?

Yes. Most insurers request both, and the bundle is cheaper than booking them separately.
