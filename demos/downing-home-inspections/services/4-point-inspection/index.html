4-Point Inspection in Orlando

Roof, electrical, plumbing and HVAC check that insurers require for older homes.

Get instant quoteCall 727-243-4158

What it is

A 4-point inspection looks at the four systems insurers worry about most: roof, electrical, plumbing and HVAC. It records the age and condition of each so the carrier can decide whether to write or renew your policy.

Who needs it

Owners and buyers of older homes (insurers often ask once a home is 20–30+ years old) who need coverage written or renewed.

What's included

  • Roof: covering type, age, remaining life, visible damage
  • Electrical: panel type, amperage, wiring type, hazards
  • Plumbing: supply and drain material, water heater age, leaks
  • HVAC: system age, operation and condition
  • Insurer-accepted form with required photos

Questions

When do I need a 4-point?

Usually when an insurer is writing or renewing a policy on an older home. Your agent will tell you if your carrier requires one.

What if something fails?

Neil will explain what was flagged and what the insurer is likely to want fixed, so you can get quotes before it delays your closing.

How fast is the report?

Insurance reports are typically delivered quickly so they don't hold up your policy. Ask about timing when you book.
