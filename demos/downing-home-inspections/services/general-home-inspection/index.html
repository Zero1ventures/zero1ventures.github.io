General Home Inspection in Orlando

Top-to-bottom inspection before you close: structure, roof, systems and more.

Get instant quoteCall 727-243-4158

What it is

A full visual inspection of the home so you know what you're buying before the inspection period runs out. You get a clear report with photos, and Neil walks you through what matters and what doesn't.

Who needs it

Homebuyers under contract, sellers who want a pre-listing inspection, and anyone who wants a clear picture of a home's condition.

What's included

  • Roof covering, flashing, gutters and visible attic framing
  • Foundation, structure, exterior walls, grading and drainage
  • Electrical panel, wiring, outlets, GFCI/AFCI protection
  • Plumbing supply, drains, water heater and visible leaks
  • HVAC system operation, age and condition
  • Interior walls, ceilings, floors, windows and doors
  • Built-in appliances, garage, and attached structures

Questions

How long does a home inspection take?

Most homes take two to four hours on site depending on size and age. Larger or older homes take longer.

Should I attend the inspection?

You're welcome to, especially for the last 30 minutes when Neil walks through the findings. It's not required; the report covers everything.

Can I add a wind mitigation or 4-point?

Yes, and it's cheaper when booked together. Most Florida insurers will ask for them before binding a policy.
