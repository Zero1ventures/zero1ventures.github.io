What this concept adds

For Neil: what changed from the current Wix site and why.

Built to turn visitors into booked jobs

  • Instant quote and booking request: visitors see a price and request a date without waiting for a callback. Every request lands in a lead inbox with the address, services, estimate and preferred date.
  • Tap-to-call everywhere: the current site blocks tap-to-call on phones. Now there's a sticky Call / Quote bar on mobile.
  • Three entry paths: buying, insurance, and new construction, each pre-selecting the right inspections and bundle pricing.
  • Insurance inspections as their own lead source: wind mitigation and 4-point get dedicated pages. Homeowners need these at renewal, separate from agent referrals.
  • One page per service and per city: replaces /blank-1 and /blank-2 so Google can rank each service in each city.
  • Trust up front: license, reviews and direct-to-Neil messaging near the top.
  • Clean-up: template placeholder text, "My Site" share name, ©2023 footer and filename alt text are gone. The unsupported "save 30%" claim is replaced with accurate wording.
  • Free Central Florida permit search: a directory of official permit search tools for Orange, Osceola, Seminole, Lake and Polk counties and their cities, with search by city or community. It gives buyers, agents and other inspectors a reason to visit and link to the site.
  • Home resources hub: flood zone lookup plus wind mitigation, 4-point and buyer timeline guides. Each one targets searches Florida buyers and owners actually make and leads to booking. Neil should review the guides before launch.
  • Search-ready: structured business data, unique titles and descriptions per page. (The demo itself is hidden from Google.)

Still needed from Neil

  • Real prices (current numbers are placeholders)
  • FL license #, insurance, certifications, years in business
  • Confirmed service area and local details for each city page
  • Logo file, headshot, truck/job photos
  • A redacted sample report for a "Sample inspection report" resource
  • Review of the resource guides
  • Google Business Profile manager access

Before going live

  • Move hosting to his domain, with redirects from the old Wix URLs
  • Text/email alert to Neil for every new lead
  • Google Analytics and call tracking to measure the before/after
  • Switch the Google profile to a service-area business
