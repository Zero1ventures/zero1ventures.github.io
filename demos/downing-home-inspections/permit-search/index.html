Central Florida building permit search

Find the right permit office for a home in Orange, Osceola, Seminole, Lake or Polk County, with direct links to each official search tool. Every link goes to the official county or city site.

First step

City or county: who has the records?

Permits are kept by whoever issued them. If the home is inside a city or town's limits, the city has the records. If it's outside any city limits (unincorporated), the county does.

A mailing address doesn't tell you. Plenty of homes with an "Orlando" or "Windermere" address are actually in unincorporated Orange County. To be sure, look the property up on the county property appraiser's site and check the taxing district or municipality listed on the record.

Tips for a better search

  • Search by address first, then by parcel number if nothing comes back. Many systems match addresses exactly, so try the house number and street name without "Dr" or "Ave".
  • Older homes often have permits in two systems. Orange County, Winter Park, Winter Haven and Seminole County all split older and newer records.
  • Not everything is online. If a roof or addition permit doesn't show up, call the office before assuming it was never permitted.
  • Some portals need a free account (Ocoee, Lakeland). Records requests are free or low cost and usually come back within a few days.

Found something that doesn't add up?

An addition with no permit, a roof older than the seller said, or an open permit from years ago? Neil can check it in person during your inspection and explain what it means for your purchase and insurance.

Buying a home?

Why permits matter before you close

Open or expired permits

A permit that was never closed out can follow the property to the new owner. Title companies and lenders may require it to be closed before closing, which can delay your date.

Unpermitted additions and conversions

Garage conversions, enclosed patios and added bathrooms done without permits may need to be permitted after the fact or removed, and insurers can deny claims tied to them.

Roof age for insurance

Florida insurers price and approve policies based on roof age, and the wind mitigation form uses the roof permit date. The permit record is the proof.

Major system replacements

Permits for AC, water heater and electrical panel work help confirm the ages listed on a 4-point inspection and show the work was inspected.
