FAQ

Common questions

How much does a home inspection cost in Orlando?

It depends on the home's size, age and which add-ons you need. Use the instant quote to see your price in under a minute; Neil confirms it before the inspection.

How soon can you inspect?

Request a date online and Neil will confirm by call or text. Tell him your inspection deadline so he can prioritize it.

Do I need a wind mitigation and a 4-point?

Most Florida insurers ask for a 4-point on older homes and give credits for wind mitigation features. Booking both together costs less.
