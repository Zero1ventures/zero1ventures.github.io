How a project runs

  1. Talk it through

    A call about what you want to build, who it's for and what has to be true on launch day.

  2. Plan

    We choose the stack, sketch the main screens and agree on what ships first.

  3. Build

    You get a working build to try every week, so feedback happens early.

  4. Launch and support

    We handle store submission and deployment, then stay on for fixes and the next round of features.
